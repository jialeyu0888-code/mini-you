## 中文

- 应用版本从 0.2.4（6）升至 0.2.5（7），沿用同一签名，覆盖安装会保留手机里已有的记录。
- 产检记录页首屏可同时看到多条最近产检记录，历史记录无需滚动即可浏览。
- 顶部卡片精简为当前孕周、下次产检日期与剩余天数；移除体重曲线卡，产检分析入口保留。
- 「记录产检」与「AI 识图」改为右下角悬浮按钮，悬浮于内容之上，AI 识图位于记录产检右侧。

## English

- The app advances from 0.2.4 (6) to 0.2.5 (7) with the same signing certificate; updating in place keeps existing records.
- The prenatal records screen shows several recent visits at once on the first screen, so history is readable without scrolling.
- The top card is streamlined to the current pregnancy week, the next visit date, and the countdown days. The weight curve card is removed while the prenatal analysis entry remains.
- "Log visit" and "AI photo scan" become floating buttons at the bottom right that overlay the content, with AI photo scan placed to the right of Log visit.
