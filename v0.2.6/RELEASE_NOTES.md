## 中文

- 应用版本从 0.2.5（7）升至 0.2.6（8），沿用同一签名，覆盖安装会保留手机里已有的记录。
- 产检记录列表显示每次产检上传的真实单据照片：多张照片可左右滑动切换列表显示图，点按可全屏预览；未上传照片的记录保留原有插画。
- 修复底部导航「首页」图标被裁切的屋顶尖并整体居中；购物清单缩略图放大，有商品图时直接显示真实照片。

## English

- The app advances from 0.2.5 (7) to 0.2.6 (8) with the same signing certificate; updating in place keeps existing records.
- The prenatal visit list now shows the real report photos uploaded for each visit: swipe to choose which photo is displayed in the list, and tap for a full-screen preview. Visits without photos keep the original illustration.
- Fixed the clipped roof tip of the Home tab icon in the bottom bar and centered it. Shopping list thumbnails are larger and show the real product photo when one is available.
