## 中文

- 对比上次本地构建 `app-release.apk`，应用版本从 0.2.3（5）升至 0.2.4（6），沿用同一签名。公开安装包已移除个人历史资料，覆盖安装会保留手机里已有的记录。
- 对比上次 GitHub 公开版 v0.2.2，日记卡片和大图预览支持左右切换照片，点底部缩略图可直接跳到对应照片。
- 日记、产检、打卡、孕肚照、个人攻略和购物商品的记录菜单统一放在右上角，可编辑或删除。移除滑动删除，删除前会再次确认。
- 支持提前安排多次产检和复诊提醒。产检分析页集中展示胎儿发育、孕期体重和血压等变化趋势。
- 购物账本增加消费分类统计、待购预算试算和未记录金额提示。孕肚记录支持填写腰围、体重并比较不同孕周的照片。
- 更新首页、日记、产检和孕育百科的界面，优化深色模式、折叠屏布局和部分表单的无障碍标签。
- GitHub 上的 v0.2.2 使用不同签名，Android 会拒绝直接覆盖该版本。请保留原应用中的资料。

## English

- Compared with the previous local `app-release.apk` build, the app advances from 0.2.3 (5) to 0.2.4 (6) with the same signing certificate. The public APK excludes bundled personal history and retains existing records when installed as an update.
- Compared with the previous public GitHub release, v0.2.2, diary cards and the full-screen photo viewer support swiping between photos. Tap any thumbnail to jump to its photo.
- Diary entries, prenatal visits, check-ins, bump photos, personal tips, and shopping items now use a top-right menu for editing and deleting records. Swipe-to-delete is removed and deletion requires confirmation.
- Schedule multiple prenatal visits with follow-up reminders. The prenatal analysis screen brings together fetal development, maternal weight, and blood pressure trends.
- The shopping ledger adds category spending totals, budget estimates for planned purchases, and missing-price indicators. Bump records support waist and weight entries plus photo comparisons across pregnancy weeks.
- Refreshed Home, Diary, Prenatal Visits, and Pregnancy Guide, with improvements to dark mode, foldable layouts, and accessibility labels on selected forms.
- The public v0.2.2 APK uses a different signing certificate, so Android rejects an in-place update from that version. Keep the records in the original installation.
